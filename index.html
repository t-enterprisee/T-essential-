Frequently asked questions

Good to know before enquiring.

Answers about how this catalogue works. Product-specific details should be confirmed directly with T-essential.

Why are prices not shown on the website?

T-essential shares prices on request. Use the product-specific WhatsApp button to ask for the current price and availability for the item you selected.

Can you share shipping costs to my country?

Send your destination country and postcode along with the product name and quantity. Shipping availability, cost and timing need to be confirmed for the particular order.

How can I confirm the material, dimensions or care instructions?

These details are not assumed on this website. Ask for the exact specifications of the product you are considering before ordering or using it for food service.

Can I ask about bulk quantities or multiple products?

Yes, you can enquire about multiple items or quantities in one WhatsApp message. Availability, minimum quantities, and any bulk terms must be confirmed directly.

Will WhatsApp show the product photo automatically?

The enquiry opens WhatsApp with the product name and a link to its individual page. WhatsApp may generate a link preview using that page's product-specific image, but preview display depends on WhatsApp. The website cannot automatically attach an image file through an ordinary click-to-chat link.

Does this website accept payments or confirm orders?

No. This site is a product catalogue and enquiry website; it has no checkout or payment collection. Confirm the final written quote, shipping terms and payment instructions directly with T-essential before paying.
